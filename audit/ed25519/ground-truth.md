# Ground truth

Checked requirements come from the requested Solana compatibility and these primary sources:

- [Agave precompile call site](https://github.com/anza-xyz/agave/blob/master/precompiles/src/ed25519.rs), manifest and lockfile: the cryptographic predicate is `PublicKey::from_bytes` followed by `verify_strict` from dalek 1.0.1/curve25519-dalek 3.2.0.
- [dalek 1.0.1 public.rs](https://github.com/dalek-cryptography/ed25519-dalek/blob/1.0.1/src/public.rs), `verify_strict`: canonical S, decompressible A/R, rejection of small-order points, SHA-512(R||A||M), and decoded-point equality. Original encoded bytes enter the hash. Mixed torsion is allowed when the full equation holds.
- [RFC 8032](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8032), sections 5.1–5.1.7: p=2^255−19, d=−121665/121666, group order8L, L=2^252+27742317777372353535851937790883648493; compressed Edwards-y encoding and SHA-512 challenge. The prime subgroup generated by B has prime order L.
- Go 1.27.1 `crypto/internal/fips140/sha512`: standard 64-bit, 80-round SHA-512 IV, round constants, schedule, padding and digest order. The circuit is compared with `crypto/sha512` across padding boundaries.
- [ZK Golf SHA-256 record](https://zk.golf/submissions/b72dabf1-4777-426d-84e8-5eb061ceda82) and [fixed-base record](https://zk.golf/submissions/1f6985a0-1b24-425d-9791-d3f62351289c): hypotheses for fused sums, lazy coordinates, packed selections and smaller arithmetic certificates. These are optimization ideas; the Ed25519 identities are checked independently by `check-identities.py`.

Exact cached source URLs, timestamps/version pins, original CCTV hash and derived fixture provenance are in `ed25519/testdata/provenance.json`. Solver validation uses the pinned Rust oracle and Go tests; external descriptions are not treated as circuit soundness evidence.
